\section{Safety, Robustness, and Ecosystem Effects}
\label{sec:safety}

Three failure modes from this toy game scale unfavorably to higher-stakes
RL deployments.

\textbf{(a) Adversarial overspecialization.} Self-play agents trained
against a small pool learn opponent-specific exploits rather than
generally robust strategies. Pure-pool agents play strongly against
neural-net opponents and weakly against the scripted baseline;
baseline-mixed agents are more general-purpose but trade off peak-vs-pool
performance.

\textbf{(b) Ecosystem effect.} The most cautionary finding is the
head-to-head between \texttt{v1\_sp\_fixed} (correctly-trained) and
\texttt{v1\_selfplay} (buggy ladder king). Across 200 matches with
side-alternation: buggy lineage 71 wins (35.5\%), fixed lineage 9 wins
(4.5\%), 120 time-cap draws (60\%) --- the buggy lineage wins
$\sim 7.9\times$ more often \emph{despite} a strictly degraded training
environment (a $\times 10$ obs-scale bug saturated the in-pool baseline's
tanh layer). The buggy lineage co-evolved against itself, so playstyles
encode mutual counters; the fixed agent, trained against the
\emph{real} baseline, behaves differently and is exploitable. A shared
training quirk becomes the local lingua franca, and a corrected
replacement model can underperform until the ecosystem
re-equilibrates --- relevant for autonomous driving fleets,
recommendation, and multi-agent market makers.

\textbf{(c) Reward hacking.} PPO reward-hacked a clean, sparse,
fully-observed reward via entropy collapse: \texttt{pool\_R}$\approx +1$
while \texttt{base\_R}$=-3.4$. The diagnostic workflow (entropy, KL,
clip-fraction monitoring with entropy-bonus and KL early-stop
intervention) is the production-realistic mitigation.

\textbf{Deployment hygiene.} Three checks before promoting any
self-play-trained policy: (i) evaluate against \emph{multiple} held-out
opponent distributions; (ii) rerun head-to-head against the previously
deployed version, since a corrected replacement may locally regress;
(iii) monitor policy entropy and KL between consecutive snapshots as
health metrics, not curiosities.
